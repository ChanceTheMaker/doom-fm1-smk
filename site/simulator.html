Play the shared Doom engine through FM-1 or SMK controls. Play the nine-level campaign with updated weapons, a classic HUD and a redesigned opening level. Click or hold the first 12 white keys, or use your computer keyboard. PATCH / PARA on SMK open and select menus. K1 controls volume; K2 scrolls menus. Drag a knob, scroll over it, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Other instrument controls are inactive. Browser speed does not measure hardware performance; progress lasts for this session.

How to play

Click Start game + sound. Use your keyboard or the first 12 white keys on either device panel. You can hold several controls together.

Find the wall switch at the end of each level and press Use. Fire or Use continues after completing a level. If you die, Use retries.

The arrow keys also move and turn. Esc opens the game menu; Enter selects. The toolbar pauses, restarts, changes the device and case color, enlarges the screen, or enters fullscreen.

Sound starts with your first interaction. Switching tabs pauses the game. No firmware installation, MIDI permission, or hardware connection is needed.
