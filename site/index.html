Before you cross over

This replaces the synth application. Back up your projects first. There are no game saves. Normal USB installation works from Doom builds with USB updating enabled, stock firmware, or a compatible synth updater. Earlier Doom builds, or an interrupted flash, require a XIAO recovery adapter before updating. Keep it available in case recovery is needed.

Release 15 is verified on FM-1. Pro and Elite hardware checks are pending for this release. Keep power connected during installation.

  1. PrepareUse desktop Chrome or Edge. Connect only your selected instrument directly by USB and leave it powered on. Close Studio and other MIDI apps.
  2. BuildChoose your exact model, then build the firmware here. SMK uses the matching official Pro 016 or Elite 016 base.
  3. InstallConfirm the notice, grant MIDI access and keep USB and power connected. Wait for the confirmed restart, then play.

Keep moving. Use opens doors. Face the wall switch in the last room and press Use to exit. Fire or Use advances from results; Use retries after death.

Menu navigation. 2 up, 4 down, 1/3 adjust, 8 select, 7 back / close. SMK PATCH opens the menu and PARA selects. Sound volume is under Options → Sound.
