Doom FM-1 Test04

FM-1 only. A silent Miniwad test room using the real Doom engine. Hardware boot has not been tested.

Doom has no USB updater. Its USB MIDI connection disappears after installation. Returning to synth firmware requires the FM-1 XIAO recovery adapter.

  1. Connect the FM-1 by normal USB, running firmware with the standard FM-1 MIDI updater. Close Studio and other MIDI apps. Leave the recovery adapter disconnected during installation.
  2. Keep power and USB connected throughout the transfer.
  3. After completion, look for DOOM TEST 04, then the room. If it stays black or shows an error, stop and report what you see.

Count white touch keys from the far left: left, forward, right, back, fire, use, menu, enter, run, strafe left, strafe right. Hold both OCT buttons for two seconds to request ROM recovery; this shortcut is also awaiting its first hardware test.
